# RIP brand guide

RIP is the Archive Toolkit: a practical, performance-focused archive utility with a custom format, compression engine, CLI, and desktop archive manager.

## Core identity

- Primary mark: rip-mark.svg — a compact geometric archive cube. Its layered front represents compacted data; the lime stripe is the recognizable brand accent.
- Primary lockup: rip-wordmark.svg — use on light backgrounds and documentation.
- Banner: rip-banner.svg — use as the README or release-page hero.
- App icon: rip.ico / rip-mark.svg — Windows executable and Qt window/taskbar icon.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon | #101828 | Primary mark and dark surfaces |
| Slate | #253247 | Layer/depth details |
| Volt lime | #C7F36B | Recognition accent and active details |
| Paper | #F4F7FB | Logo light strokes and dark-background type |
| Slate text | #596579 | Secondary wordmark type on light backgrounds |

## Usage

- Keep the mark's proportions and line weights intact. Prefer the SVG source where supported.
- Use rip-wordmark.svg on light surfaces and rip-banner.svg on dark surfaces.
- Maintain clear space of at least one lime-stripe width around the mark.
- Do not stretch, recolor the lime stripe, add shadows, or place the dark mark on a similarly dark unframed background.
- Use RIP Archive Toolkit in long-form product descriptions and RIP in compact UI/icon contexts.
